Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Metro Linkz Corp and MCNS

What makes Metro Coin different from cryptocurrency?

Metro Coin is NOT blockchain-based and NOT a cryptocurrency. It operates on Token6 — Metro Linkz's proprietary digital value ledger designed for speed, security, and zero external blockchain dependency. This gives Metro Linkz Corp full control over the currency while ensuring scalability and stability for Africa's digital economy.

How can I start earning with Lean Business?

To join Lean Business, you can apply through our official platform. Successful applicants receive training, tools, and resources to become MCNS agents. You'll execute marketing tasks, build communities, create content, and promote MCNS products — earning rewards through Leancnstask that can be withdrawn via Metro Wallet.

What is the Token6 Ledger System?

Token6 is Metro Linkz's proprietary digital value ledger designed specifically for Metro Coin. It provides the speed and security of blockchain technology without the limitations of external blockchain dependencies. Token6 allows for full control, scalability, and customized economic operations tailored to Africa's digital growth needs.

How does Metro Pay differ from other payment systems?

Metro Pay is uniquely integrated into the entire MCNS ecosystem. Unlike traditional fintechs that require external user adoption, Metro Pay grows through MCNS platforms, Metro Linkz products, and revenue shares from partner ecosystems. This creates a self-powered financial engine that doesn't depend on VC funding or unstable scaling.

What is the long-term vision for MCNS?

MCNS aims to become Africa's dominant digital economy ecosystem, integrating AI, digital currency, fintech, cloud systems, and digital work infrastructure. Our vision is to position Africa as a creator and owner of digital innovation rather than just a consumer, transforming the continent's economic landscape and global technological influence.
